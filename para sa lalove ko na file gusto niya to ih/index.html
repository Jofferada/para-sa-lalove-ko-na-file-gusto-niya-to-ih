My heart knew it.

When I ask, "Do you still love me?" it is not because I want to doubt you. It is because my heart is soft around you, and sometimes it wants to hear the sweetest truth from the person who matters most.

I love the way you make ordinary days feel warmer. I love the little moments, the quiet care, the jokes, the comfort, and the feeling that even a simple message from you can make my whole day gentler.

If your answer is yes, then please know this: I still choose you too. I choose you in the happy moments, in the quiet ones, in the days when we are silly, and in the days when we need patience. I choose your smile, your heart, your presence, and the version of me that feels safe being loved by you.

Thank you for staying in my heart. Thank you for making love feel cute, playful, emotional, and real. I hope this little page reminds you that you are deeply loved, missed, treasured, and kept close in every tiny corner of my heart.

So yes, my love, I still love you. Always a little more than yesterday.
